# Q0–Q3 reasoning model

## Q0 — Situation and boundary

Define the case, target learner, known facts, missing prerequisites, uncertainty and scope.

## Q1 — Mechanism

Reconstruct observation → intermediate physiology → predicted findings; test necessary premises and counterexamples.

## Q2 — Decision

Translate mechanisms into conditional choices with population, timing, benefits, harms and evidence. Separate source facts from editorial inference.

## Q3 — Reassessment

Identify new evidence that would reverse a premise, propagate its consequences across related units and test transfer to unfamiliar cases.

Concept, Decision, Evidence and Navigation are four projections of the same causal structure, not separate disconnected documents.
