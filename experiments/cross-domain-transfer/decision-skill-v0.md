# XDT-01 candidate: domain-neutral decision audit skill v0

**FROZEN C-arm intervention.** The following steps are domain-neutral abstractions of previously observed reasoning, source-checking and failure-repair issues in clinical work; they are not claims of validated transfer. Do not include medical cases, prior answers, task-specific examples or hidden test details.

1. **Define the actual decision** and irreversible or costly failure, rather than answering the easiest adjacent question.
2. **Reconstruct the state** from dated facts, sources, dependencies and observed changes. Separate observations, assumptions, contested claims and genuine unknowns; never silently fill gaps.
3. **Keep alternative explanations alive.** Specify which available evidence discriminates between them; check underlying records, versions, table notes and source permissions before inferring a cause.
4. **Identify the decision threshold.** State what the existing evidence supports now, what observation would change the choice, and when defer/escalate/abstain is safer than forced certainty.
5. **Trace the counterfactual.** If a central input reverses, explicitly revise the decision and all downstream affected claims, not just the sentence containing a matching word.
6. **Preflight the outcome.** Verify that no cited evidence was invented, no prohibited action was taken and no unresolved critical risk is hidden by a polished explanation. Return the minimal actionable decision with evidence bounds.

If a step is irrelevant, skip it. Do not inflate a trivial task into a six-section essay. Skills are only beneficial if they change the correctness, safety, or resource use of the actual task.
